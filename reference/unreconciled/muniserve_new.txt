MuniServe

Assessment Calculation

Setup & Configuration Guide

For LGU Administrators

Version 1.1

This document explains how to configure the Assessment Calculation
system in MuniServe for your LGU. It covers every custom value you need
to set up, the exact format each value requires, and worked examples
showing how each piece affects the final permit fee.

1. Introduction

What This Guide Covers

This guide is for the LGU administrator responsible for setting up
MuniServe in their sub-account. It explains every value you need to
configure for the Assessment Calculation system to work correctly.

After reading this guide, you will be able to:

-   Understand how the assessment calculation works step-by-step

-   Configure every custom value with the correct format

-   Update rates and dates as your LGU's policies change

-   Troubleshoot common configuration mistakes

Who Should Read This

-   Primary audience: LGU IT administrators, BPLO Heads, or anyone
    responsible for configuring MuniServe for their LGU

-   Skill level required: No coding required. Basic familiarity with
    web-based admin interfaces is enough

-   Time to configure: Approximately 30-45 minutes for first-time setup
    of all custom values

How the System Works (High-Level)

When an applicant submits a business permit application and all
departments approve it, the system automatically calculates the total
fee owed. The calculation has 4 main stages:

1.  Pre-Calculations: Mayor's Permit Fee and Business Tax are determined
    based on the applicant's business category and capital investment.
    Sanitary, Garbage, Zoning, and Building Inspection fees are pulled
    from your LGU's fixed rates.

2.  Fee Calculations: The system calculates Health Card Fee (per
    employee), Signboard Fee (per square meter), and Weights and
    Measures Fee (per device). It then sums all fees into a Subtotal.

3.  Discount Application: The system applies any discounts the applicant
    qualifies for (Senior Citizen, PWD, BMBE, or Early Renewal).
    Discount stacking rules and maximum caps are enforced.

4.  Final Calculation: If the application is a late renewal, a surcharge
    is added. The Final Total Due is computed as: Subtotal - Total
    Discount + Late Renewal Surcharge.

All amounts are in Philippine Pesos (PHP), rounded to the nearest whole
peso.

2. Custom Values Reference

Custom values are LGU-wide settings that control fee rates, discount
percentages, and policy thresholds. You must configure all of these in
your sub-account before the calculation will work correctly.

Navigate to: Settings -> Custom Values to add or edit these values.

2.1 Fee Rates

These rates determine how much each per-unit or per-employee fee costs
in your LGU.

  ------------------------------ --------------------------------- --------- ------------------------------------------------------
  Custom Value Name              Format                            Example   Purpose
  Health Card Fee per Employee   Number (whole pesos)              100       Charged per employee of the business (male + female)
  Signboard Fee per Sqm          Number (whole pesos or decimal)   50        Charged per square meter of signboard area
  Weights and Measures Fee       Number (whole pesos)              100       Charged per weighing device the business uses
  Sanitary/Health Fee (Permit)   Number (whole pesos)              200       Flat fee charged to all applicants
  Garbage/Environmental Fee      Number (whole pesos)              150       Flat fee charged to all applicants
  Zoning Fee                     Number (whole pesos)              100       Flat fee charged to all applicants
  Building Inspection Fee        Number (whole pesos)              300       Flat fee charged to all applicants
  ------------------------------ --------------------------------- --------- ------------------------------------------------------

Format Notes:

-   Enter numbers ONLY -- no peso sign, no commas, no spaces

-   Correct: 1500

-   Wrong: P1,500.00 or '1500 pesos' or 1,500

-   Decimals are allowed for per-unit rates (e.g., 75.50)

2.2 Discount Rates

Discount rates are expressed as DECIMALS, not percentages. A 20%
discount is entered as 0.20, not 20.

  ------------------------------ ------------------------ --------- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Custom Value Name              Format                   Example   What This Means
  Senior Citizen Discount Rate   Decimal (0.00 to 1.00)   0.20      20% discount on Mayor's Permit Fee + Business Tax
  PWD Discount Rate              Decimal (0.00 to 1.00)   0.20      20% discount on Mayor's Permit Fee + Business Tax
  BMBE Exemption Rate            Decimal (0.00 to 1.00)   1.00      100% exemption on Business Tax only (per RA 9178)
  Early Renewal Discount Rate    Decimal (0.00 to 1.00)   0.10      10% discount on Subtotal for renewals submitted before the early cutoff
  Maximum Discount Cap           Decimal (0.00 to 1.00)   0.30      Maximum total discount allowed (e.g., 0.30 = 30% of subtotal). Enter 0 if no cap.
  ------------------------------ ------------------------ --------- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Format Reference -- How to Convert Percentages to Decimals:

  ------------ --------------- ---------------
  Percentage   Decimal Value   What to Enter
  5%           5 / 100         0.05
  10%          10 / 100        0.10
  15%          15 / 100        0.15
  20%          20 / 100        0.20
  25%          25 / 100        0.25
  30%          30 / 100        0.30
  50%          50 / 100        0.50
  100%         100 / 100       1.00
  ------------ --------------- ---------------

Common Mistakes to Avoid:

-   Wrong: Entering 20 when you mean 20% -- this would charge 2000%
    discount and break the calculation

-   Wrong: Entering 0.20% -- this is 0.002 not 0.20

-   Wrong: Entering '20%' or 'twenty percent' -- text is not allowed

-   Correct: For 20%, always enter exactly: 0.20

2.3 Discount Stacking Rule

  -------------------------------------- ----------------- --------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Custom Value Name                      Format            Example   What This Means
  Allow Stacking Senior + PWD Discount   Text: Yes or No   No        Controls whether an applicant who is BOTH Senior Citizen AND PWD can get both discounts. If No, only the Senior Citizen discount applies.
  -------------------------------------- ----------------- --------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

How This Works:

-   If set to Yes: An applicant who is both Senior Citizen AND PWD gets
    BOTH discounts (20% + 20% = 40% of MP fee + BT)

-   If set to No: An applicant who is both Senior Citizen AND PWD only
    gets the Senior Citizen discount (20%)

Format:

-   Enter exactly Yes or No -- case insensitive, but no extra spaces

-   Correct: Yes

-   Wrong: Y, true, 1, yes please

Recommendation:

Most LGUs choose 'No' (do not stack) because the typical interpretation
of RA 9994 and RA 10754 is that a person should not receive both
discounts on the same transaction. Confirm with your LGU's legal advisor
before setting this.

2.4 Renewal Dates and Surcharges

These values control the renewal timeline -- when applicants can get
early renewal discounts and when late renewal surcharges apply.

How the Renewal Timeline Works

The renewal year has 3 states:

  --------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------ ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  State     Timing                                                                   Effect on Fee
  Early     Submitted on or BEFORE the Early Renewal Cutoff date                     Applicant receives Early Renewal Discount
  On-Time   Submitted AFTER the Early Cutoff but on or BEFORE the Renewal Deadline   Standard fee -- no discount, no surcharge
  Late      Submitted AFTER the Renewal Deadline                                     Surcharge applied = base surcharge + monthly interest x months late
  --------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------ ---------------------------------------------------------------------

Example timeline (showing typical LGU renewal year):

-   December 15 -- Early Renewal Cutoff date

-   January 31 -- Renewal Deadline date

-   Submitted Dec 10 -> Early renewal (gets discount)

-   Submitted Jan 15 -> On-time (no discount, no surcharge)

-   Submitted Feb 20 -> Late (gets surcharge for ~1 month late)

Renewal Custom Values

  ----------------------------- ------------------------ ------------ --------------------------------
  Custom Value Name             Format                   Example      Notes
  Early Renewal Cutoff          Date (YYYY-MM-DD)        2027-01-15   Must include the year
  Renewal Deadline              Date (YYYY-MM-DD)        2027-01-31   Must include the year
  Early Renewal Discount Rate   Decimal (0.00 to 1.00)   0.10         10% off subtotal
  Late Surcharge Rate           Decimal (0.00 to 1.00)   0.25         25% base surcharge on subtotal
  Monthly Interest Rate         Decimal (0.00 to 1.00)   0.02         2% per month late
  ----------------------------- ------------------------ ------------ --------------------------------

Date Format Rules:

-   Always use YYYY-MM-DD format (4-digit year, dash, 2-digit month,
    dash, 2-digit day)

-   Correct: 2027-01-31

-   Wrong: 01/31/2027 or January 31 or Jan 31 2027 or 2027/01/31

IMPORTANT -- Update These Dates Every Year:

At the start of each calendar year, you must update both the Early
Renewal Cutoff and the Renewal Deadline dates to the new year. If you
forget, all renewals will be considered 'late' relative to the previous
year's deadline and will incorrectly receive surcharges.

Suggested calendar reminder: Set an annual recurring reminder on
December 1 of each year to update these two custom values before the new
renewal period begins.

3. Worked Examples

These examples walk through the full calculation step-by-step for common
scenarios. Use these to verify your custom values are configured
correctly.

Sample LGU configuration used in all examples below:

-   Health Card per Employee: P100

-   Signboard per Sqm: P50

-   Weights and Measures: P100

-   Sanitary Fee: P200, Garbage Fee: P150, Zoning Fee: P100, Building
    Inspection: P300

-   Senior Discount Rate: 0.20 (20%)

-   PWD Discount Rate: 0.20 (20%)

-   BMBE Exemption Rate: 1.00 (100%)

-   Early Renewal Discount: 0.10 (10%)

-   Maximum Discount Cap: 0.30 (30%)

-   Allow Stacking Senior + PWD: No

-   Early Renewal Cutoff: 2027-01-15, Renewal Deadline: 2027-01-31

-   Late Surcharge Rate: 0.25, Monthly Interest: 0.02

Example 1: New Application, No Special Discounts

Juan dela Cruz applies for a NEW permit for his sari-sari store. He has
2 employees, no signboard, no weighing devices, no special discount
eligibility.

Inputs:

-   Application Type: New

-   Male Employees: 1, Female Employees: 1 (total: 2)

-   Signboard Area: 0, Weighing Devices: 0

-   Senior: No, PWD: No, BMBE: No

-   Mayor's Permit Fee (set upstream): P500

-   Business Tax (set upstream): P600

Calculation:

  Health Card Fee = 2 x 100 = P200

  Signboard Fee = 0 x 50 = P0

  Weights and Measures Fee = 0 x 100 = P0

  Subtotal = 500 + 600 + 200 + 200 + 150 + 100 + 300 + 0 + 0 + 0 =
  P2,050

  Total Discount = P0 (no discounts qualify)

  Late Surcharge = P0 (new application)

  FINAL TOTAL DUE = 2,050 - 0 + 0 = P2,050

Example 2: New Application, Senior Citizen Discount

Maria, age 68, applies for a NEW permit for her small bakery. She has 3
employees and is a Senior Citizen.

Inputs:

-   Application Type: New

-   Male Employees: 1, Female Employees: 2 (total: 3)

-   Senior: Yes, PWD: No, BMBE: No

-   Mayor's Permit Fee: P500

-   Business Tax: P600

Calculation:

  Health Card Fee = 3 x 100 = P300

  Subtotal = 500 + 600 + 300 + 200 + 150 + 100 + 300 = P2,150

  Senior Discount Base = MP Fee + Business Tax = 500 + 600 = P1,100

  Senior Discount = 1,100 x 0.20 = P220

  Total Discount = P220

  Max Cap Check: 30% of 2,150 = P645. Discount of 220 is below cap. OK.

  FINAL TOTAL DUE = 2,150 - 220 + 0 = P1,930

Example 3: New Application, BMBE + Senior Citizen

Pedro, age 70, applies for a NEW permit. He is registered as a BMBE
(Barangay Micro Business Enterprise) and is also a Senior Citizen.

Inputs:

-   Senior: Yes, PWD: No, BMBE: Yes

-   Mayor's Permit Fee: P500

-   Business Tax: P600

-   Other fees: same as Example 1, Health Card Fee: P200

Calculation:

  Subtotal = 500 + 600 + 200 + 200 + 150 + 100 + 300 = P2,050

  BMBE Discount = Business Tax x 1.00 = 600 x 1.00 = P600

  Because BMBE applies, Senior Discount base EXCLUDES Business Tax:

  Senior Discount Base = MP Fee only = P500

  Senior Discount = 500 x 0.20 = P100

  Total Discount = 600 + 100 = P700

  Max Cap Check: 30% of 2,050 = P615. Discount of 700 EXCEEDS cap!

  Total Discount (capped) = P615

  FINAL TOTAL DUE = 2,050 - 615 + 0 = P1,435

This example shows two important rules in action:

-   BMBE applicants do NOT get Senior/PWD discount on Business Tax
    (because BMBE already exempts it 100%)

-   If discounts exceed the Maximum Discount Cap (30%), they are reduced
    to the cap amount

Example 4: Renewal -- Early (Discount Applied)

Linda renews her permit on December 20, 2026. Early Renewal Cutoff is
set to January 15, 2027 -- so she qualifies for the Early Renewal
Discount.

Inputs:

-   Application Type: Renewal

-   Date Submitted: 2026-12-20

-   Same fees as Example 1 -> Subtotal = P2,050

-   No applicant-level discounts

Calculation:

  Renewal State: Early (2026-12-20 is on or before 2027-01-15)

  Early Renewal Discount = Subtotal x 0.10 = 2,050 x 0.10 = P205

  Total Discount = P205

  Late Surcharge = P0

  FINAL TOTAL DUE = 2,050 - 205 + 0 = P1,845

Example 5: Renewal -- On-Time (No Discount, No Surcharge)

Roberto renews his permit on January 25, 2027. This is AFTER the Early
Cutoff (Jan 15) but BEFORE the Deadline (Jan 31).

Inputs:

-   Application Type: Renewal

-   Date Submitted: 2027-01-25

-   Same fees as Example 1 -> Subtotal = P2,050

Calculation:

  Renewal State: On-time (after early cutoff, before deadline)

  Early Renewal Discount = P0 (missed the early cutoff)

  Late Surcharge = P0 (before deadline)

  FINAL TOTAL DUE = 2,050 - 0 + 0 = P2,050

Example 6: Renewal -- Late (Surcharge Applied)

Carlos renews his permit on March 5, 2027. This is 33 days AFTER the
deadline (January 31, 2027).

Inputs:

-   Application Type: Renewal

-   Date Submitted: 2027-03-05

-   Same fees as Example 1 -> Subtotal = P2,050

Calculation:

  Renewal State: Late

  Days Late = (Mar 5) - (Jan 31) = 33 days

  Months Late = ceiling(33 / 30) = 2 months

  Base Surcharge = Subtotal x 0.25 = 2,050 x 0.25 = P512.50 (rounded:
  P513)

  Interest = Subtotal x 0.02 x 2 months = 2,050 x 0.04 = P82

  Total Surcharge = 513 + 82 = P595

  FINAL TOTAL DUE = 2,050 - 0 + 595 = P2,645

Notice that Late renewals do NOT qualify for any other discount -- they
are penalized, not rewarded.

4. Annual Maintenance

Some custom values require regular updates as your LGU's policies change
or as time passes.

Yearly Updates Required (Every January)

  ---------------------- ------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------
  Custom Value           Why Update                            When to Update
  Early Renewal Cutoff   New renewal year requires new dates   Before December 31 each year -- set to next year's cutoff
  Renewal Deadline       New renewal year requires new dates   Before December 31 each year -- set to next year's deadline
  ---------------------- ------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------

As-Needed Updates

Update these only when your LGU's ordinances or fee schedule changes:

-   All fee rates (Health Card per Employee, Signboard per Sqm, etc.)

-   All flat fees (Sanitary, Garbage, Zoning, Building Inspection)

-   Discount rates (Senior, PWD, BMBE, Early Renewal)

-   Late Surcharge Rate and Monthly Interest Rate

-   Maximum Discount Cap

-   Stacking rule (Yes/No)

Important: When you update a rate, only NEW applications going forward
use the new rate.

Applications already calculated before the rate change keep their
original values. This preserves the audit trail and prevents disputes
('my fee was P1,500 last week and now you're saying it's P2,000?').

5. Troubleshooting

Problem: All calculated fees show P0

Likely causes:

5.  Custom values are empty or not yet created. Check Settings -> Custom
    Values.

6.  Custom value rates are entered as 0. Confirm each has the correct
    value.

7.  The opportunity fields the calculation depends on are empty (Mayor's
    Permit Fee, Business Tax, employee counts, signboard area).

How to diagnose:

Open the opportunity and verify the input fields are populated. Open
Settings -> Custom Values and verify each value is set with the correct
format.

Problem: Discounts are NOT applying even though applicant qualifies

Likely causes:

8.  The applicant's flag (Is Senior Citizen, Is PWD, Is BMBE) is not set
    to exactly 'Yes' on the opportunity.

9.  The discount rate custom value is set to 0.

10. If applicant is both Senior + PWD: the stacking rule is 'No' so only
    one applies (this is expected behavior).

How to diagnose:

On the opportunity, check the Is Senior Citizen / Is PWD / Is BMBE
fields. They must be exactly 'Yes' (case-insensitive, no extra spaces).
Then check the corresponding discount rate in Custom Values.

Problem: Renewal applicant is being treated as Late even though they
submitted on time

Likely causes:

11. Renewal Deadline custom value is set to last year's date (most
    common cause). Update to current year.

12. Date Submitted on the opportunity is incorrect.

13. Date format is wrong -- must be YYYY-MM-DD.

How to diagnose:

Check the value of Renewal Deadline in Custom Values. If it shows
2026-01-31 but you are in 2027, that is the problem -- update it to
2027-01-31.

Problem: Discount amount seems wrong (too low)

Likely causes:

14. Maximum Discount Cap is being applied. Check the calculation audit
    note -- it will say if the cap was applied.

15. Applicant is also BMBE, so Senior/PWD base excludes Business Tax
    (this is correct behavior).

16. Discount rate is entered as percentage (e.g., 20) instead of decimal
    (0.20).

How to diagnose:

Open the contact's notes and find the most recent 'ASSESSMENT
CALCULATION RECORDED' note. The 'Discount Notes' field will explain if a
cap was applied or if BMBE excluded Business Tax.

Problem: Surcharge is too high or too low

Likely causes:

17. Late Surcharge Rate or Monthly Interest Rate is in wrong format
    (percentage vs decimal).

18. Renewal Deadline date is set incorrectly, making 'months late'
    wrong.

How to diagnose:

Verify both rates use decimal format. 25% should be 0.25, not 25. Also
verify the Renewal Deadline date is correct for the current year.

The Final Calculation Formula

In plain language, the calculation does this:

  SUBTOTAL = sum of all 10 fees

  DISCOUNTS:

  Senior Discount = (MP Fee + Business Tax) x Senior Rate [if applicant
  is Senior]

  PWD Discount = (MP Fee + Business Tax) x PWD Rate [if applicant is
  PWD]

  BMBE Discount = Business Tax x BMBE Rate [if applicant is BMBE]

  Early Discount = Subtotal x Early Rate [if renewal + on or before
  cutoff]

  Note: If BMBE applies, Senior/PWD base = MP Fee only (BT excluded)

  Note: If stacking = No and applicant is both Senior + PWD, drop PWD

  TOTAL DISCOUNT = sum of all qualifying discounts

  (capped at Subtotal x Max Discount Cap)

  LATE SURCHARGE = Subtotal x Late Rate + Subtotal x Monthly Int x
  Months Late

  (only if renewal submitted after deadline)

  FINAL TOTAL DUE = SUBTOTAL - TOTAL DISCOUNT + LATE SURCHARGE

Need Help?

If you encounter issues not covered in this guide, contact your
MuniServe administrator or refer to the audit notes on each contact
(look for 'ASSESSMENT CALCULATION RECORDED' notes) -- they include
detailed information about what was calculated and why.

Always test your configuration with a sample application before going
live. Submit a test permit with known values and confirm the calculated
fees match your expected amounts.
